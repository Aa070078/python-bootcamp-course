PART 2 // BRANCHES
🌿 CONCEPT
Branches = parallel timelines
Your project's official story lives on main. A branch lets you explore a new idea on a separate timeline — without breaking the main story. When the idea works, you merge it back.
🌿
Why branches rock
• Experiment freely — main stays safe
• Multiple features developed at once
• Teams work without collisions
🛡️
The golden rule
Never build directly on main when experimenting. Make a branch, try, then merge if it's good.
🎬
For your level
We keep it simple today: create a branch, switch, commit, merge. No advanced strategies.
main = official feature branch = experiment
