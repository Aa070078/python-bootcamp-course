Why care?
Readability: f(city="Cairo", age=19) is self-documenting. Order stops mattering.
Rules 📏
✅ f(x=1)
✅ f(1, y=2)
❌ f(y=2, 1) # positional after keyword
Keyword args = passing by name, not by position.
