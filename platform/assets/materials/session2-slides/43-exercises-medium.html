01
Leap Year?
Ask for a year. Divisible by 4 AND (not by 100 OR by 400) → leap year. Use and/or.
Uses: if/elif/else, logical operators, %
02
Ticket Price
Under 12: free. 12–17: $10. 18–59: $25. 60+: $15. Ask age, print price.
Uses: if/elif/else (4 branches)
03
Day of the Week
Ask for a number (1–7). Print the day name. Handle invalid numbers.
Uses: if/elif/else (7 branches), input validation
04
Is it a Vowel?
Ask for a letter. Print "Vowel" or "Consonant". Handle uppercase too.
Uses: if/elif/else, membership in, .lower()
