Arithmetic Operators.
Do math on numbers.
العمليات الحسابية — الرياضة اللي عرفناها من الابتدائي
+
Addition
10 + 3 → 13
−
Subtraction
10 - 3 → 7
*
Multiplication
10 * 3 → 30
/
Division (always float!)
10 / 3 → 3.333...
//
Integer division
10 // 3 → 3
%
Modulo (remainder)
10 % 3 → 1
**
Power (exponent)
2 ** 10 → 1024   ·   5 ** 2 → 25
// ORDER MATTERS
Python follows math rules:
1. ** power first
2. * / // % next
3. + - last  ·  use ( ) to override
