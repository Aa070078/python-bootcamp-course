🎯 MCQ — Pick the right module
You need to print today's date and time, formatted like 2026-08-03 14:30. Which module is the right tool?
A  random B  datetime C  math D  statistics
🔍 random → chance & shuffles. math → numbers. statistics → averages. None touch clocks.

⏰ datetime owns dates & times. Quick win: from datetime import datetime
    print(datetime.now().strftime("%Y-%m-%d %H:%M"))
✔ Answer: B — datetime handles dates & times.
وحدة datetime هي الأداة الصحيحة للتواريخ والأوقات، مع strftime لتنسيقها.
