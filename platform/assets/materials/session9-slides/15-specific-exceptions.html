CORE // 04 — KNOW YOUR EXCEPTIONS
🔑 The exceptions you'll meet daily
Each exception = a specific accident. Catch the specific type, not everything.
ValueError
Right type, wrong value. int("banana") 😵
TypeError
Wrong type. "2" + 2 🤝
IndexError
Out of range. nums[99] 🎯
KeyError
Missing dict key. data["age"] 🔑
FileNotFoundError
File missing. open("nope.txt") 📁
ZeroDivisionError
Math on zero. 10 / 0 ➗
🐍 Bonus: Exception is the parent of ALL of these. Catching it = catching everything (use with care!).
