Build a username formatter step by step
01 Remove leading/trailing spaces
02 Convert to lowercase
03 Replace spaces with underscores
04 Remove special characters
main.py 
username = "  Ahmed Ali  "

# Step 1: Strip spaces
username = username.strip()

# Step 2: Lowercase
username = username.lower()

# Step 3: Replace spaces
username = username.replace(" ", "_")

print(username)  # "ahmed_ali"
Input: " Ahmed Ali " Output: "ahmed_ali"
