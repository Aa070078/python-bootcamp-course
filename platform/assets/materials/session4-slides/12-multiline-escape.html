Escape Characters:
main.py 
# Special escape characters
print("Line 1\nLine 2")
# Line 1
# Line 2

print("Name\tAge")
# Name    Age

print("Path: C:\\Users")
# Path: C:\Users

print("She said \'hi\'")
\n = newline
\t = tab
\\ = backslash
\' = single quote
