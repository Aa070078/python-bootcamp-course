3 ways to build dynamic strings
formatting.py 
name = "Ahmed"
age = 20

# 1. Concatenation (old way)
msg1 = "I am " + name + " and I am " + str(age) + " years old"

# 2. .format() method
msg2 = "I am {} and I am {} years old".format(name, age)

# 3. f-String (modern — preferred!)
msg3 = f"I am {name} and I am {age} years old"

print(msg3)  # "I am Ahmed and I am 20 years old"
💡 f-Strings are the preferred modern approach — clean, readable, and fast!
