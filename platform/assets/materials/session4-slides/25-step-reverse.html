STEP & REVERSE
الخطوة والعكس
main.py 
word = "Python"

# Step of 2 (every 2nd char)
print(word[::2])      # Pto

# Reverse the entire string
print(word[::-1])       # nohtyP

# Reverse every 2nd character
print(word[::-2])       # nhy

# Slice then reverse
print(word[1:5][::-1])  # ohty
🔥 Common Interview Trick
Reversing a string:
word[::-1]
Palindrome check:
word == word[::-1]
✓ This is one of the most common Python interview questions!
