🛡️ CONCEPT 07 — CONTEXT MANAGER
with — the safe way
`with` opens the file, gives you a clean block, and closes it automatically — even if an error happens mid-block. No more manual close().
❌ OLD WAY — easy to forget
f = open("x.txt", "w")
f.write("hi")
f.close()  # 😴 easy to forget…
✅ NEW WAY — always safe
with open("x.txt", "w") as f:
    f.write("hi")
# closes itself after the block 🎉
👑 Rule: always open files with with from now on.
