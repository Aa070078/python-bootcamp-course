🔑 CONCEPT 01 — WHY PERSISTENCE MATTERS
Why persist data?
💀
Variables die with the program
Lists, dicts, counters — all live in RAM and vanish on exit.
💾
Files live on the disk
Text files, images, databases — they survive restarts and reboots.
⚡
Persistence = superpower
Settings, game saves, orders, logins — the heart of every real app.
"Persistence turns a demo script into real software."
