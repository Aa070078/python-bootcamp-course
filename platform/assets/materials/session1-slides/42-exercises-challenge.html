For students who want more. Not required — but proud if you finish.
تحديات — مش إجبارية، بس اللي يعملها فخور
C1
BMI Calculator
Ask for weight (kg) and height (m). Compute BMI: weight / height². Print rounded to 1 decimal.
Uses: float() · ** power · round() · f-string formatting
C2
Countdown to Graduation
Ask for current grade (1–12). Print exact years, months (× 12), days (× 365) left until grade 12.
Uses: multiple calculations from one input
C3
Time-to-Seconds Machine
Ask for hours, minutes, seconds. Convert to total seconds. Then print the reverse: given a total, break it back down.
Uses: multiplication · modulo (%) · integer division (//)
C4
"Beautiful" Business Card
Extend Student Card: add borders with "═" * 40, emoji per subject, and align columns with padding.
Uses: string repetition · aesthetics
