EXERCISES // EASY — WARM UP
Exercises
EASY
5–10 minutes each. Warm-ups. Everyone should finish these.
تمارين سهلة — إحماء، كل واحد يقدر يحلها
E1
Hello, You
Ask the user for their name. Print "Hello, [name]!" back to them.
Uses: input · print · f-string
E2
Sum of Two
Ask for two numbers. Print their sum, difference, and product.
Uses: input · int() · math operators
E3
Age in Days
Ask for the user's age. Print how old they are in days (roughly). Hint: age × 365.
Uses: input · int() · multiplication · f-string
E4
The Full Sentence
Ask for name, city, and hobby. Print: "[name] lives in [city] and loves [hobby]."
Uses: 3 variables · f-string
