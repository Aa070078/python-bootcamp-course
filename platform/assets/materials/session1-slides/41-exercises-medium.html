Temperature Converter
Ask for a temperature in Celsius. Convert to Fahrenheit. Formula: F = C × 9/5 + 32
Uses: float() · math · f-string
M2
Bill Splitter
Ask for total bill and number of people. Print how much each person pays.
Uses: float() · int() · division · f-string
M3
Rectangle Detective
Ask for width and height. Print area and perimeter. Bonus: also print if it's a square.
Uses: int() · math · == comparison → bool
M4
Name Statistics
Ask for the user's full name. Print: how many letters (incl. spaces), in UPPERCASE, and in lowercase.
Uses: len() · .upper() · .lower()
