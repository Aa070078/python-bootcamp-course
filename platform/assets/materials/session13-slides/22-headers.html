🧾 CORE CONCEPT #9
Headers
The "meta" envelope around the message — labels, not content. Both requests and responses carry them.
REQUEST HEADERS (YOU SEND)
User-Agent: my-weather-bot/1.0
Accept: application/json
X-Api-Key: ••••••••
Who you are · what format you accept · your key.
RESPONSE HEADERS (SERVER RETURNS)
Content-Type: application/json
Date: Mon, 03 Aug 2026
RateLimit-Remaining: 99
Format · when it was sent · how many requests left.
Today you mostly send User-Agent and X-Api-Key (slide 32). The library sets the rest for you.
