🧮 SET OPERATIONS
Four superpowers: | & - ^
Venn-style thinking — a = {1,2,3} · b = {3,4,5}
UNION 🧬
a | b
→ {1, 2, 3, 4, 5}
INTERSECTION 🎯
a & b
→ {3}
DIFFERENCE ➖
a - b
→ {1, 2} (in a, not in b)
SYMMETRIC DIFFERENCE 🪞
a ^ b
→ {1, 2, 4, 5} (in exactly one)
