🔁 ITERATION
Looping Over a Dictionary
See the keys, the values, or both — pair by pair.
التكرار على القاموس: نستطيع المرور على المفاتيح أو القيم أو كلاهما معًا في حلقة for.
loop.py
scores = {"Ali": 90, "Lina": 85, "Omar": 78}
 
print(scores.keys()) # dict_keys(['Ali', 'Lina', 'Omar'])
print(scores.values()) # dict_values([90, 85, 78])
print(scores.items()) # dict_items([('Ali', 90), ...])
 
for name, score in scores.items():
    print(name, "got", score)
 
# Ali got 90 · Lina got 85 · Omar got 78
THREE VIEWS
🔑  .keys()
Just the names — loop over keys only
💎  .values()
Just the numbers — loop over values only
🧶  .items()
(key, value) pairs — unpack both in the loop
🤖  Pro tip
for k in d: is a shortcut for for k in d.keys():
