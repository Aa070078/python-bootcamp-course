CORE CONCEPT // RESPONSIBILITY ⚖️
Legal & Ethical Basics
الأساسيات القانونية والأخلاقية للكشط: قراءة ما هو عام، لا اختراق، لا إلحاق ضرر
✅
Always OK
Public pages, small volumes, your own data, well-known sandbox sites (quotes.toscrape.com, books.toscrape.com).
⚠️
Check first
Read robots.txt + Terms of Service. Respect rate limits. Ask: "would I be OK if someone did this to my site?"
🚫
Never
Personal/private data, login-gated content, bypassing CAPTCHAs or anti-bot systems, flooding servers, re-selling data.
🗝️ Golden rule: behave like a polite reader, not a thief. We teach respectful scraping only.
