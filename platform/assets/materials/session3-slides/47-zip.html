LOOP + DATA // ZIP — PARALLEL ITERATION
الحلقات والبيانات — التكرار المتوازي
zip-example.py 
# Loop two lists together
names  = ["Ali", "Sara", "Omar"]
scores = [85, 92, 78]

for name, score in zip(names, scores):
    print(f"{name}: {score}")
report.py 
# Student report card
students = ["Ali", "Sara", "Omar"]
math    = [85, 92, 78]
english = [90, 88, 95]

for s, m, e in zip(students, math, english):
    avg = (m + e) / 2
    print(f"{s}: avg={avg}")
🔗 zip() pairs elements from multiple lists — stops at the shortest one.
